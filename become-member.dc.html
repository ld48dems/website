Become a member of LD 48

Become a member of the 48th Legislative District Democrats and help elect candidates who share our values.

As a member you have a voice in our endorsements, which also help shape King County Democrats endorsements.

2026 is a pivotal year, with races up and down the ballot including five State Supreme Court Justice seats.

Voting membership for eligible people begins 20 days after signing up via the form below.

Questions? Contact Catherine at ld48demsmembership@outlook.com or the Chair at 48thDems@gmail.com.

No donation is required to become a member. Suggested donations are $48 annually per person or $25 for Young Dems. Monthly donations are welcome. Donate here.
