Become a PCO

A precinct committee officer, or PCO, is the elected face of the Democratic Party in your neighborhood. It is one of the most direct ways to make a difference.

What a PCO does

PCOs are the grassroots organizing base of the Democratic Party. They know their precinct, help turn out the vote, and represent their neighbors within the district party.

  • Canvass or telephone the precinct in coordination with election strategy at least once a year.
  • Coordinate with campaigns to educate voters and generate interest in candidates and ballot measures.
  • Attend legislative district and county organization meetings.
